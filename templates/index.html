🕉️ What is Yoga Mudra?

Yoga Mudras are ancient hand and body gestures used in yogic practices to direct the flow of life energy (prana) within the body. Rooted in Ayurveda and Indian spirituality, these mudras are believed to balance the five elements — earth, water, fire, air, and space — which are associated with different parts of the body and mind.

By forming specific shapes with fingers or through body postures, mudras stimulate acupressure points, activate subtle energy pathways (nadis), and harmonize the mind-body connection. Regular practice of mudras can help manage chronic health conditions like stress, diabetes, hypertension, and respiratory issues.

Yoga Mudras are simple to perform, require no equipment, and can be practiced by anyone, anywhere — making them a powerful tool for holistic healing and self-transformation.

🧠 Gyan Mudra

Benefit: Improves focus, memory & reduces anxiety

How: Touch tip of index finger to tip of thumb. Extend other fingers.

🌬️ Vayu Mudra

Benefit: Relieves gas, arthritis, joint pain

How: Fold index finger to thumb base. Press thumb over it.

💧 Varun Mudra

Benefit: Increases hydration, improves skin texture

How: Touch tip of little finger to thumb tip. Keep other fingers relaxed.

🔥 Surya Mudra

Benefit: Boosts metabolism and supports weight loss

How: Bend ring finger to base of thumb and press lightly.

🌿 Prana Mudra

Benefit: Increases vitality, reduces fatigue and enhances immunity

🧹 Apana Mudra

🔥 Agni Mudra
